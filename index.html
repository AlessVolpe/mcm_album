That was all a burning memory: a concept

Language is a structure. It is through deconstruction, pun intended, that we humans can examine how meaning is constructed, and how it can fail. Music is also a Language. Poetry is a Language, or the artistic use of it. And what is art, if not another Language.

In a Platonic sense, Ideas are pure, ideal Forms. What we experience in our physical world is imperfect, and what we make of it may be nothing more than a silhouette or a shadow. Ideas, most of the time, fail us. Deleuze challenges the traditional model of representation: difference precedes identity, and the things we experience cannot always be reduced to fixed Forms.

Humans use Language to convey Ideas. Society is built around the failure of Ideas; all of our problems, as a species, are failing Ideas. Capitalism and the patriarchy, as systems, are the epitome of failure, they produce and can’t live without exploitation, which brings the annihilation of Language. Their continued reproduction also reveals our failure to question and dismantle the ideology that sustains them. What’s left to us, mere spectators? Art and Love, Languages.

Here comes the title of this concept: what happens when Ideas fail, destroying the Language of Art and Love? Memory burns, the Body burns with it, they die annihilated by its most important function; Body is what defines a human, if it dies, even metaphysically, the human also dies. In this Language of Love, there’s a Subject and an Object. We experience the other through desires and projections. The Lacanian concept of objet petit a, the object-cause of desire, helps to describe how something unattainable can organize our longing, even when the person we desire exists independently of our fantasies. Trying to contain it within Ideas leads to failure. Failure is what brought this to life.

In this specific case, the subject is the narrator, who tried to force the object into a triad:

  • Jouissance, the enjoyment that exceeds ordinary pleasure, becoming excessive, painful or even intolerable;
  • Cristallisation, described by Stendhal as a process in the birth of love, the mind idealizes another person until, in this particular case, they become a motionless image in the mind of the subject;
  • The Light, a term I use to express what remains of Love when it has taken form through shared life, and what keeps it from being forgotten. A warm light.

It’s time to read the first story, with all the joy and disgust I had in that moment.

I instantly have to correct myself, everything happened from spring 2025 through summer of 2026. Ideas fail to represent reality.

I went back to Rome. Now it has a place too, it’s going to be important later. Besides I’ve already spoken about the absolute pinnacle of Roman engineering.

It was probably, a year before everything happened. I remember meeting this girl: beautiful and ethereal, like any other girl you meet late at night at your favorite pub or on the metro while having a panic attack. She always came around closing hours. She would get a small lager or two, maybe sometimes talk with anyone who was around there (sometimes it was me), almost no-one being most of the time close to 2AM. She looked lonely.

We’d spoken casually before. Last March, I approached her. I wanted to know her better. I was alone too. After that evening we didn’t meet for a long time, at the Aphelion we met again.

In the days, weeks, months between March and July, she had become a motionless image in my mind: as beautiful as every time I met her and as ethereal as she was every time I went to the pub thinking about she suddenly sitting at her table.

Most of the time, she would never come. I wrote Aphelion when that Image was on the brink of destruction. And just after that it happened, I snapped out of it, she made me. By being alive, and still being the most difficult and distant person I ever got to know. That’s how a crystal forms. It takes the shape of that same person that generates it, but it’s a simulacrum of the object of desire. It’s a contradiction, platonically, the Idea of a contradiction; its purest form, but it, again, fails to represent the real thing.
It’s an echo chamber, it feeds itself, it grows and then fails under its own weight.

The Decristalisation is painful, but rapid: Harey (I’m going to stop warning) is annihilated, and she’s reduced to a light gust of wind; my crystal? Tells me to snap the fuck out of it.

“There is a light that never goes out”

I would say that this is more than a mere quote from that racist wank that is Morrissey, but hey! he sure wrote some amazing lyrics while yearning for Marr’s love. A light that never goes out, is it just saying for “there’s always hope”, is it really talking about someone that gets in your life and just keeps shining light at you?

I hope that Morrissey shuts the fuck up about this song and never talks about it. But I digress. If there’s a glue to everything I said in this incoherent and deliberately delirious document, it is trauma. It’s a story about losing your light and having no idea about how to fix your mess of a life, because you’re scared of anything that goes out of your previously established ordinary. It’s a vicious circle, because it will always repeat itself until you (I, I’ve been projecting everything on no-one, the reader) change for good.

At the start I’ve talked about a capitalized The Light, something that keeps love from being forgotten. As much as anyone would think that this is a positive term (light must be good), it’s not. In this case, it’s what keeps me from going on. As much as anyone would like to turn off their emotions, it’s impossible. I’m tautological, but it is what it is. The next poem is an act of Hauntology (more in the sense that i feel like this is something that Boards of Canada would do), it’s a Frankenstein, some of the passages are translated and adapted from L’Ombra delle Colline by Giovanni Arpino. This novel came at the right time in my life to completely fuck me up, and it did. the fact that a woman from a novel, an ephemeral presence in my recent life, and my ex-partner all line up for some reason, completely fabricated and unbelievable at the same time. That’s what happened though.

Connection through a name, then through how she acted while listening to my autistic rambles, through music; it all boiled down to this.
